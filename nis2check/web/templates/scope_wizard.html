Are you in scope of NIS2?

An indicative check against Directive (EU) 2022/2555, Articles 2 and 3, and the size ceilings in Recommendation 2003/361/EC. {{ rules.disclaimer }}

Annex I is sectors of high criticality. Annex II is other critical sectors. The types of entity follow the annex, including the exclusions written into it.

Medium is 50 or more employees, or turnover and balance sheet both above EUR 10 million. Large is 250 or more employees, or turnover above EUR 50 million and a balance sheet above EUR 43 million. A figure that only meets one of the two financial ceilings stays in the lower band.
