Appendix B. Methodology

Each requirement is broken down into practical controls, and each control is given a regulatory priority: critical (weight 3), high (weight 2) or medium (weight 1). This baseline is then raised by up to 50% for controls that counter threats common in recent incident data: the uplift is proportional to the combined prevalence of the threat patterns the control counters, relative to the most exposed control. Threat data can raise a weight but never lower it below the regulatory baseline, because every control is a legal requirement. Sources and links are listed in Appendix C. Answers are scored as met = 1, partially met = 0.5, not met = 0 and not assessed = 0. Controls marked not applicable are excluded.

The overall and per-requirement scores are weighted averages of the applicable controls. The evidence-backed score applies the same calculation but counts a control only when every evidence item recorded for it exists. Open items are ranked by control weight multiplied by missing coverage, so an unaddressed critical control ranks above a partially met medium one.

Effort is an estimate (low, medium, high) of the work needed for an SME to implement the control. Risk reduction per unit of effort divides the risk reduction by 1, 2 or 3 respectively and drives the impact vs effort matrix.

Maturity bands: strong (90% and above), substantial (70 to 89%), partial (40 to 69%), low (below 40%).

This report is a self-assessment and does not constitute a certification, audit opinion or legal advice. National transposition laws, implementing acts and guidance from the competent authority may impose additional or more specific requirements.
